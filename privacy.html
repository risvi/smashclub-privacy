Smash Club — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-07

Smash Club ("the app", "we") is a badminton club management app published by Risvi Yoosuf. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. Read it before signing up. If you don't agree, don't use the app.

1. What we collect

When you use Smash Club, we store the following on our servers (operated by Supabase):

  • Email address — used to sign in and reset your password.
  • Phone number — used to identify you when other club members add you to a league or session roster.
  • Display name — the name shown to other members.
  • Profile photo (optional) — uploaded by you, stored in Supabase Storage.
  • App activity you create — leagues, sessions, matches, scores, and court-fee ledger entries.
  • Device push token (optional) — used to send notifications about sessions and matches you're attending. Only collected if you grant notification permission.

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and profile picture from Google's OAuth response — nothing more. We do not request access to your Google contacts, calendar, or any other Google data.

2. How we use it

The data above is used solely to operate the app: authenticate you, show you your data and your club's data, and notify you about activity that involves you. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or anything else.

3. What we don't collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your device's contacts, photos (other than ones you explicitly upload), microphone, or camera.
  • We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or behavioural telemetry.
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third-party advertisers, brokers, or analytics providers.

The only third parties that can access your data are the infrastructure providers we use to run the service:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, storage, realtime sync. Data stays inside Supabase's hosted PostgreSQL.
  • Google Cloud / Firebase Cloud Messaging — only if you enable push notifications. We send a device push token to FCM so it can deliver notifications to your device.
  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose "Continue with Google" to sign in.

These providers process data solely on our behalf, under their own privacy policies and security commitments.

5. How long we keep your data

Account data is kept as long as your account is active. App activity (leagues, sessions, matches you've created) stays until you delete it from the app or delete your account. Soft-deleted records are kept for 30 days to allow undo, then permanently removed.

6. Your rights

You can:

  • Edit your name, phone number, and profile photo at any time from Profile → Edit.
  • Delete individual leagues, sessions, and matches from inside the app.
  • Sign out from Profile → Sign Out.
  • Request full account deletion (including all data tied to your account) by emailing the address below. We honour deletion requests within 7 days.

7. Children

Smash Club is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data at rest is encrypted by Supabase using industry-standard practices. Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth and never visible to us.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top and notify you in the app on next sign-in. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or anything else: m.yoosuf@mag.global
